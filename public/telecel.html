Telecel bundles without the queue

Telecel (formerly Vodafone) bundles are delivered automatically after payment. Member prices are shown first; guests see the guest price struck through and can sign in to save. Most orders arrive within minutes. Double-check the beneficiary number. Wrong numbers cannot be reversed.

Broadband and corporate Telecel SIMs are not eligible. Ported numbers keep their old prefix, so check the current network before choosing a bundle.

Telecel FAQ

How fast is Telecel delivery?

Usually minutes. When the Telecel network is congested it can take longer. Check the live status banner on this page, and track your order with the reference we send you.

Can I buy a Telecel bundle for someone else?

Yes. Enter any valid Telecel beneficiary number at checkout.

Which Telecel numbers can receive these bundles?

Standard Telecel prepaid numbers on 020 and 050, including numbers ported in from other networks. Broadband and corporate Telecel SIMs are not eligible.

Does a ported number still work?

Yes, but a ported number keeps its original prefix, so it will look like the network it came from. Always choose the bundle for the network the SIM is currently on.
