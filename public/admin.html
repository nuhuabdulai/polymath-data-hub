    How to approve an order

    Pending payment means a customer paid by MoMo or cash and sent you proof: once you have the money press Mark paid & send and the data goes out at once. Card payment not confirmed means their card never went through, so ask them to pay by MoMo and then press Mark paid & send (they paid another way). Card payments that work confirm themselves, so leave those alone. Nobody paid? Press Cancel; it works on both unpaid states, anything unpaid for 24 hours clears itself, and an order that is In progress or Delivered can no longer be cancelled.

    Matching a payment to an order

    The Paystack ref is the one Paystack emailed the customer and the one you search for in your Paystack dashboard. The Supplier ref is the supplier's own order number, only useful if you need to ask them about a specific delivery. They are different numbers for the same order, so No card payment means the customer paid by MoMo or cash and never used Paystack. Use the search box to find an order by either reference, the order number, the phone or the tracking code.
