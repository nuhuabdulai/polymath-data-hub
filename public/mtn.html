Why buy MTN data here?

Polymath Data Hub buys MTN bundles at reseller rates and passes the saving to you. You pay the member price shown in green (guest price is struck through). Delivery is automated, with no waiting for a vendor to send it manually. Most MTN orders complete within minutes. MTN's own system is often congested, so an order can take hours. We watch every order and step in by hand if one stalls, and you can track the status with your reference. A queued order is not a failed order, so it is not refunded while it is still in the queue.

Enter the beneficiary number carefully. Data sent to a wrong or newly activated number cannot be reversed by the carrier. Ported numbers keep their old prefix, so a 024 number that was ported to Telecel will still look like MTN. Always choose the bundle for the network the SIM is currently on.

Turbonet, broadband and corporate MTN SIMs are not eligible for these bundles. If you are unsure, message us on WhatsApp before paying.

MTN FAQ

How fast is MTN delivery?

Usually minutes. When MTN is congested it can take hours. Check the live status banner on this page, and track your order with the reference we send you.

Can I buy an MTN bundle for someone else?

Yes. Enter any valid MTN beneficiary number at checkout, including ported numbers that keep their old prefix.

Which MTN numbers can receive these bundles?

Standard MTN prepaid numbers, including ported ones. The checkout recognises 024, 025, 053, 054, 055 and 059. Turbonet, broadband and corporate MTN SIMs are not eligible.

What if I enter the wrong number?

Data sent to a wrong or newly activated number cannot be reversed by the carrier, so please check the number before paying. If an order fails on our side, we refund it - see the refund policy.
