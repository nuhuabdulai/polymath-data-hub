Why buy MTN data here?

Polymath Data Hub buys MTN bundles at reseller rates and passes the saving to you. You pay the member price shown in green (guest price is struck through). Delivery is automated, with no waiting for a vendor to send it manually. Most MTN orders complete within minutes. MTN's own system is often congested, so an order can take hours. We keep retrying and you can track the status with your reference. A queued order is not a failed order, so it is not refunded while it is still in the queue.

Enter the beneficiary number carefully. Data sent to a wrong or newly activated number cannot be reversed by the carrier. Ported numbers keep their old prefix, so a 024 number that was ported to Telecel will still look like MTN. Always choose the bundle for the network the SIM is currently on.

Turbonet, broadband and corporate MTN SIMs are not eligible for these bundles. If you are unsure, message us on WhatsApp before paying.

MTN FAQ

How fast is MTN delivery?

Can I buy MTN bundle for someone else?

Yes, any valid MTN number.
