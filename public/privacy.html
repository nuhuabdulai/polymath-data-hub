Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 Sep 2026

This page explains what personal information POLYMATH DATA HUB collects, why we need it, who else sees it, and what you can ask us to do with it. It is written to be read, not to be skipped.

Who we are

POLYMATH DATA HUB is a data bundle reseller based in Accra, Ghana, and we are responsible for the personal information described here. You can reach us using the WhatsApp number or email address in the footer of any page on this site.

What we collect

  • The number you are sending data to. We cannot deliver a bundle without it.
  • Your own phone number, and your email address if you give us one — to create your account, send your one-time code, and send receipts.
  • Order details: the bundle, the network, the amount, the reference, the status and the times.
  • Wallet balance and top-up history, if you use the wallet.
  • Payment confirmation from Paystack — the amount and a reference. We never see or store your card number, because the card is entered on Paystack's own page.
  • A session cookie so you stay signed in.
  • If you choose to sign in with Google: your name, your email address and your Google account ID.

We do not run advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not sell your information.

Why we collect it

  • To deliver the bundle to the number you entered.
  • To take payment, and to pay refunds.
  • To let you track an order and see your purchase history.
  • To send one-time codes and receipts.
  • To prevent fraud, duplicate orders and abuse of the service.
  • To keep the accounting records the law expects us to keep.

Who we share it with

We keep this to the companies that are needed to make the service work:

  • iDATA, our data supplier, receives the beneficiary number, the network, the bundle and an order reference. Without this the bundle cannot be delivered at all.
  • Paystack processes card payments and receives the amount and a reference.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) delivers your one-time code and order notifications.
  • Our SMS provider sends your one-time code when WhatsApp is unavailable.
  • Google, and only if you choose to sign in with Google.

Some of these providers process data outside Ghana. Where that happens we rely on those providers' own safeguards for international transfers. We do not share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Order and wallet history are kept while your account is active, and afterwards for as long as we need them for accounting, tax and dispute records. Your session cookie lasts 30 days, or until you sign out. You can ask us to delete your account and your personal information at any time: we will delete everything we are not required by law to keep.

Cookies

We use httpOnly session cookies (30 days) to keep you signed in, and a shorter-lived one for admin sign-in. There are no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking cookies on this site.

Your rights

Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you, and give you a copy of it;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your information;
  • stop processing it, or withdraw consent you gave earlier.

Message us on WhatsApp or email us using the details in the footer and we will act on it. If you are not satisfied with how we handle your request, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana.

How we protect it

All traffic to this site is encrypted with TLS. Passwords are stored only as scrypt hashes with a per-user salt, never as plain text. Sessions live in httpOnly cookies that scripts on the page cannot read. Admin access is password-protected and rate-limited. Card payments are handled entirely by Paystack, so card details never reach our servers.

Children

This service is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use the service after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about your data, or a request under Act 843? Use the WhatsApp number or email address in the footer of any page and we will respond.
