Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 Sep 2026

What we collect

Phone numbers, order references, and (optional) email. No card data is stored. Paystack handles payments.

Why

To deliver bundles, track orders, prevent fraud, and send receipts via SMS/WhatsApp if you opt in.

Retention

Orders and wallet history kept while your account is active. You can request deletion via WhatsApp/email.

Cookies

We use httpOnly session cookies (30 days) to keep you logged in. No tracking cookies.
