Track your order

No account needed. Type the tracking code from your order confirmation and you will see exactly where your data is.

It looks like PD-ABC123XYZ0 and is shown on your confirmation screen.

Only needed if you are entering an order reference that starts YB.

Cannot find your code?

  • PD- codes come straight from your confirmation screen or your WhatsApp receipt message.
  • If you only have the order reference (it starts YB), add the last 4 digits of the number the data is going to.
  • Lost both? Message us on WhatsApp with your number and we will look it up for you.

Delivery is usually minutes. When a network is congested it can take hours. A slow order is not a failed order.
