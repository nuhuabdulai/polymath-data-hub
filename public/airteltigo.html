How fast is AirtelTigo delivery?

Usually minutes. When the network is congested it can take longer. Check the live status banner on this page, and track your order with the reference we send you.

Can I buy an AirtelTigo bundle for someone else?

Which AirtelTigo numbers can receive these bundles?

What if I enter the wrong number?
