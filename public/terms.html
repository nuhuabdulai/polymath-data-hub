Terms of Service

Last updated 26 Sep 2026

1. Service

We resell mobile data bundles for MTN, Telecel and AirtelTigo in Ghana. Delivery is automated through our delivery service. Delivery times are estimates, not a guarantee: a network can be congested and take hours. We show the live status of each network on the homepage. If it says delayed, budget for hours.

2. No reversal

Data sent to a wrong, inactive or ported number cannot be reversed or refunded. The carrier treats it as delivered. You must confirm the beneficiary number before paying (large confirmation step shown at checkout).

3. Ineligible SIMs and numbers

Data is not delivered, and cannot be reversed or refunded, if the beneficiary number is any of these:

  • Agent, merchant or EVD SIM
  • Turbonet, broadband or WiFi-router SIM
  • Blacklisted, roaming or inactive SIM
  • A SIM with borrow credit or borrowed data. Your data will be wiped once it completes.
  • A number on a different network to the bundle you bought
  • A wrong or mistyped number

If you are unsure, ask us on WhatsApp first. We would rather check than lose the order.

4. One order per number at a time

Please do not place two orders for the same number at the same time. One of them can be rejected at the network with no refund, and we cannot recover that. Wait for your order to complete before ordering again for the same number.

5. Payments

We accept wallet, Paystack (MoMo/card) and manual MoMo. Orders are processed after payment confirmation.

6. Support

Contact us on WhatsApp or email shown in the footer. Ported numbers keep their old prefix. Check the bundle network, not just the prefix.
